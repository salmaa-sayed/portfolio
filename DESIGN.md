# Salma Sayed — Made to move

The visual identity is built around animation's language: paths, keyframes, a
playhead, and purposeful movement. The hero pairs kinetic display typography
with Salma's own motion work in offset frames. It introduces what she does
before the portfolio's quiet, easy-to-browse media rows.

Palette: charcoal #171717, white #f4f4f4, frost #f1f1f5, muted grey
#b0b0bb, deep wine #8f2938, red #bd495b, and soft rose #e7a4ae.
White headlines lead the charcoal surfaces. Deep red grounds the main button,
play controls, and secondary frame; lighter reds mark paths and interaction
states. Small text stays white or grey for readable contrast.
Type: Bricolage Grotesque for expressive display; Manrope for reading and
controls. Both are self-hosted Latin WOFF2 with their OFL licenses.
Layout: centered navigation, left-aligned desktop text, centered mobile text,
generous whitespace, a landscape motion row and
portrait teaser/reel rails. Each frame has its own caption and playback
controls. Footer becomes a quiet charcoal and wine contact canvas.
Motion: one quiet hero playhead sequence; interactions show clear state
changes. Reduced-motion disables the decorative sequence and autoplay.

The functional reference contributes media behaviors only. No reference
styles, personal copy, hero imagery, or portfolio media are used.
